Connect Jira

Three things from your Jira account. Oriel keeps them in your Mac's keychain, with your other passwords.

Atlassian lets you make these for apps like Oriel. One has your Jira access and no more, Oriel changes Jira only when you approve, and you can delete it on Atlassian's page any time.

  1. Open Atlassian's page for them (it opens in your browser).
  2. Click Create, name it Oriel, pick when it expires (a year is fine), and click Create.
  3. Click Copy, then paste it above. Atlassian shows it only once.
