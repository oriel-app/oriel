Sign in to Claude Code yourself

If signing in from Oriel doesn't work, you can do it in Terminal, the Mac's app for typing commands. It takes a minute, and you only do it once.

  1. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, and press Return.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal, and press Return:
    claude auth login
  3. Your browser opens claude.ai. Sign in and click Authorize. If the page shows a code, copy it and paste it into Terminal.
  4. When Terminal says Login successful, come back here.
