# 0010 No DCO

Accepted 2026-10-28. Changes SPEC §5 and §10.

**Decision.** No Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off and no sign-off check.

**Why.** Process overhead without a demonstrated need. Comparable agent-heavy open-source projects don't require it. CONTRIBUTING.md keeps the two lines that matter: the human who runs an agent owns what it submits, and contributors confirm no employer claim.

**Revisit** if a contribution's provenance is ever questioned, or a foundation or company adopting Oriel requires it.
