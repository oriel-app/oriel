## Foundations

Tokens, type, space, motion, focus, icons and layout.

- [Colour](guide/foundations/colour.md): Oriel's colour tokens by role, in Graphite and Broadsheet, light and dark. The accent means "your move", five states stay apart, and every pair is checked.
- [Type](guide/foundations/type.md): System fonts only, a five-step scale from --text-xs to --text-xl, three weights and three leadings. Serif for the thread, panel and page titles; every rem follows the Text size setting.
- [Space, shape and elevation](guide/foundations/space.md): The space scale, row density per theme, three radii, two border widths, two shadows and the scrim. Plus the layer scale, what stacks over what.
- [Motion](guide/foundations/motion.md): Three durations and two easings. Quick for a state, move for a place, morph and the spring for a flourish. When things move and when they never do, how a drag, a menu and an arrival move, one pulse for work in progress, and one reduced-motion block that stills every move.
- [Focus](guide/foundations/focus.md): One focus ring, in the accent, 2px out. Where Oriel overrides it today, the five looks its fields have, and the one field-frame focus proposed.
- [Icons](guide/foundations/icons.md): A small set of 16px outline icons drawn inline, stroked in the text's colour. Words first; an icon that's the only label needs an aria-label.
- [Layout and density](guide/foundations/layout.md): The shell (a sidebar beside the page, with the panel or window chat over or beside it), its width tokens, one screen breakpoint at 760px, container queries for the page, and the narrow 390 view.

## Patterns

How components combine for one job.

- [Approval](guide/patterns/approval.md): The human-in-the-loop moment. The agent proposes a change set; only a person approves it, in the thread or on a review link, and nothing is written to a source until they do.
- [Destructive actions and undo](guide/patterns/destructive-actions.md): Undo for what Oriel can give back; one question, with a danger button, only where Undo can't reach. Remove, mark done, delete a workstream with its threads, disconnect.
- [Long lists](guide/patterns/long-lists.md): Avoid a long list before scrolling it. Show the top few with "N more", page in place with "Show 50 more", and search before reaching for a virtual list. Never infinite scroll.
- [Forms and validation](guide/patterns/forms.md): Labels that say what to type, notes beside the field, a submit that waits for what's required, the server's one sentence on submit, and the field it's about marked invalid. No field is marked or tied to its error today.
- [Connect steps](guide/patterns/connect-steps.md): One component draws a connector's steps, in the connector's own words, wherever a connection is made in the window, which since decision 0059 is only a folder; and one review step draws a connection's mapping, to apply or edit, in Settings. Everything else comes through the person's own source servers.
- [Drop to add](guide/patterns/drop-zone.md): Dragging a card, a link, an item's key or a file onto a workstream or one of its threads adds it to the workstream; a veil over the page says where the drop goes.
- [Drag to move](guide/patterns/drag-to-move.md): An item's row dragged onto a workstream in the sidebar moves it there; onto the Workstreams heading, a menu offers a new workstream. A row's menu does the same from the keyboard.

## Controls lab

Proposed form controls in modern CSS, beside today's.

- [Select](guide/lab/select.md): **proposed**. One select for Browse's filters, "Add to…" and the model pickers, as a customizable select (appearance base-select) in Oriel's own picker, with a native fallback. Classes: `.dropdown` `.dropdown-dot` `.dropdown-text` `.dropdown-label` `.dropdown-about` `.dropdown-count` `.filter` `.filters` `.add-to` `.select` `.settings` `.settings-field` `.settings-field-name` `.settings-field-about` `.visually-hidden`.
- [Checkbox and radio](guide/lab/checkbox-radio.md): **proposed**. Oriel's checkboxes and radios are the browser's, tinted with accent-color. A drawn version with appearance none, beside them in every state, and why accent-color is enough for now. Classes: `.checkbox` `.radio` `.pick` `.anchor-pick` `.choice` `.choices`.
- [Switch](guide/lab/switch.md): **proposed**. Today's switch is a button with role switch. Three ways to build one, compared, and a redraw whose off state can be seen in every theme and whose locked state can be reached by keyboard. Keep the button. Classes: `.switch-track` `.switch` `.switch-knob` `.settings-switch` `.settings-field` `.settings-field-name` `.settings-field-about`.
- [Text field](guide/lab/text-field.md): **proposed**. One Field (label, hint and problem tied with aria-describedby) for Oriel's eight field styles, textareas that grow with field-sizing, :user-invalid beside aria-invalid, and one SearchField. Classes: `.text-field` `.text-field-label` `.text-field-hint` `.text-field-problem` `.text-field-input` `.search-field` `.search-field-input` `.path-form` `.path-label` `.path-note` `.connect-problem` `.input` `.settings` `.provider-path-label` `.held-message` `.held-by` `.held-field` `.name-field` `.reason` `.link-field` `.sidebar-search-field` `.filter-search` `.icon` `.visually-hidden`.
- [Popover, menu and tooltip](guide/lab/popover.md): **proposed**. Oriel's menus and tooltips are popovers that JS measures and places. CSS anchor positioning places them instead, flips them at an edge, and fades them in; interestfor and popover=hint can open a tooltip without JS in the desktop app. The context menu at the pointer stays JS. Classes: `.popover-menu` `.popover-tip` `.context-menu` `.context-menu-item` `.tooltip` `.head-action` `.panel-close`.
- [More modern CSS](guide/lab/more.md): **proposed**. The rest of modern CSS, judged for Oriel one feature at a time: light-dark(), color-mix(), @starting-style, interpolate-size, @scope, container queries, text-wrap, scrollbar-gutter, :has(), nesting, @layer and sibling-index(). Classes: `.more-unfold` `.more-enter` `.more-balance` `.more-pretty` `.more-scroll` `.more-scroll-row` `.more-stagger` `.more-scheme` `.run` `.run-body` `.code` `.output` `.context-menu` `.context-menu-item` `.button` `.page-title` `.panel-prose` `.ask-questions` `.ask-question`.
